High-Pressure Quantum Sensing

Academic Year 2025–2026

I worked on an experimental quantum-sensing platform using nitrogen-vacancy centers in diamond anvil cells. My work focused on synchronizing a wide-field camera system with laser pulse sequences used to measure materials under high pressure.

Summer 2026

Conducted through Harvard’s Program for Research in Science and Engineering (PRISE) .

I switched to an atomic, molecular, and optical physics subgroup working with ultracold-atom experiments. I worked on laser frequency stabilization by developing a scanning transfer-cavity control system and implementing data acquisition and feedback for stable laser operation.

RIKEN Center for Brain Science

June–August 2025

Wako, Saitama, Japan

Advisors: Dr. Takuya Isomura and Dr. Richard Servajean

Participated in the RIKEN CBS Summer Program through Harvard’s RIJS Japan Summer Science Undergraduate Research Program (JSSURP) .

I conducted theoretical-neuroscience research on the Free Energy Principle. I extended the standard mathematical framework of internal and external states, separated by sensory and active states, to hierarchical models. I developed the corresponding mathematical formalism, conducted computational simulations, and presented the results at the end of the internship.

MIT 3D Optical Systems Group

February–May 2025

Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Cambridge, Massachusetts

Advisor: Professor George Barbastathis

I studied a computational model of a solution containing two types of microparticles. I extended an initial model to incorporate more complex and realistic particle dynamics, generated simulated speckle images, calculated and analyzed image autocorrelations, and fitted several models to the resulting autocorrelation data.
